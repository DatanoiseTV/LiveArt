Keyboard Shortcuts

  • 1-9: Increase parameters
  • Shift+1-9: Decrease parameters
  • Space: Randomize parameters
  • → / ←: Change preset
  • F: Fullscreen
  • H: Hide controls
  • M: Show/hide MIDI mappings
  • L: Toggle Live Mode (hide all UI)

Your settings are automatically saved and restored when you reopen the app. You can also save named presets with the Save/Load buttons.
